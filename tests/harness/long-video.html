2.4.7: what the page does with a long saved video, timed in a real Chrome with the extension's own code. Opening a saved video: the repairs ytRepairCachedLeaks applies on load (sound notes, head repeats, slid runs, leaks) and the re-cut of what changed, the merge of every cue (ytGhepViCues), then the dubbing plan for one planning window (dub-engine refreshSource: planStep 60 s, 150 s behind, 300 s ahead) and, as an upper bound, for the whole video. Each step is one synchronous task: over ~200 ms the tab would stutter.
